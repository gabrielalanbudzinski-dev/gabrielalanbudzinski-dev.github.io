I’m Gabriel Budzinski, a Game Designer specializing in UGC platforms and player-focused product design. Over the last six years, I’ve worked across game design, level design, systems, economies, prototyping and project leadership.

My advantage is understanding both the creative side of a game and the reasons players actually stay. I like breaking down player behavior, identifying friction, designing strong loops and translating ideas into systems that teams can build.
