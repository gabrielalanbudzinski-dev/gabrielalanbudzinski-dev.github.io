Tuning Cars Tycoon

A car customization and tycoon experience developed in just 2 months with a low budget and a team of 4.

I was the sole Game Designer, leading the initial platform research by analyzing trending experiences and player behavior, then designing the product end-to-end: core gameplay loop, mechanics, progression, economy and systems.

To make development predictable for a small team, I created balancing spreadsheets, technical documentation and highly visual design documents. I also designed the complete UI/UX, building the initial wireframes and flows before handing them to an artist for final production.

The result was a focused product that moved from concept to implementation with minimal iteration overhead and reached more than 2 million unique players.

Soccer Tycoon

My first Tycoon project on the platform, developed in partnership with Level 8.

I worked directly with the partner, gathering feedback and translating requirements into the product vision. I designed the experience end-to-end, including the pitch, core loop, mechanics, progression, economy and systems.

Shortly before launch, a professional football club expressed interest. Because the product had been designed as a white-label experience, we integrated the brand without changing the core gameplay, while I led the adaptation of its identity and player-facing interactions.

The launch reached a 19K+ all time peak and a 40-minute average session, turning the project into one of the strongest-performing products in the portfolio.

Surf Tycoon

A surf-themed Tycoon centered on building a Surf Camp, combined with an endless runner built around tricks and high scores.

I owned the project end-to-end across product design, purchase flows, economy balancing, progression, level design, technical documentation and core gameplay systems.

Developed and published in only 4 weeks, it was structured as a white-label product with the goal of integrating a potential brand. No partnership was secured before launch, which limited the available content and scalability.

Even with the constrained production window, the experience reached 800K+ players. With additional time and stronger brand integration, I believe its retention and long-term performance could have been improved substantially.

Stonebridge — Dungeon Siege

A narrative-driven experience developed for The Sandbox in collaboration with the Dungeon Siege brand.

The project was inherited from another studio in a partially completed state. Our team had to rebuild and fix most of the existing experience while preserving its core vision and the lore of the original 2002 game.

I contributed by designing and implementing new dialogues, quests, narrative content, gameplay systems and logic needed to make the world and its interactions function cohesively within The Sandbox.

Despite the inherited technical and design challenges, we brought the experience to completion in approximately one month and published it in The Sandbox Alpha.
